Current requirement test - 16 in crude line, Kharif gathering station to the Sahl junction

Setting A: GB-1 2.10 A, GB-2 2.30 A
Setting B: GB-1 3.60 A, GB-2 3.20 A

Native survey: all rectifiers off for 21 days before the survey.
ON/OFF surveys: every rectifier switched by a GPS-synchronised interrupter, 4 s on / 1 s off;
the OFF value is read 300 ms after the switch-off.

Survey days and electrode checks (field electrode minus reference cell, read at the start of each day):
  native survey: day 1 0-12000 m +1 mV | day 2 12000-24000 m 0 mV | day 3 24000-36000 m -1 mV
  setting A: day 1 0-12500 m 0 mV | day 2 12500-25000 m +2 mV | day 3 25000-36000 m +1 mV
  setting B: day 1 0-12000 m -1 mV | day 2 12000-24500 m +1 mV | day 3 24500-36000 m 0 mV
